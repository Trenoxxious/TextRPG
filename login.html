Welcome to a complex, but simple-to-start, space-themed text RPG game! Planet Earth has been desolated by nuclear warfare, The Human Race has been granted asylum on Planet Nalyd under one condition: you must survive Space Cadet Camp to prove you are an asset to the Nalydian people! Gather Materials, build weapons and fight hostile creatures to be granted survival!
